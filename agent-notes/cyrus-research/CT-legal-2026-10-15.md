## QUESTIONS the owner must decide (I did not invent answers)
1. **Jurisdiction / governing law and venue.** The Nxeon Terms literally say "the jurisdiction in which we are established ... should be finalized with legal counsel". Cyrus Terms point to it. Needs a real clause (and the legal entity name and registered postal address, which appear nowhere in the codebase; GDPR and Google verification both like a postal address).
2. **Contact email.** Using `support_email` (support@nxeon.cloud). Want a dedicated privacy@ / legal@ / abuse@ address and a DPO/EU representative statement?
3. **Refund policy for Cyrus tokens and plans.** The text says refunds follow Nxeon terms and checkout, spent tokens are not refunded, and reversal on refunds/chargebacks. Decide: any money-back window for unused token top-ups, plan cancellation refund, and the stance on dissatisfied-with-AI-result.
4. **Token expiry numbers.** Text says "expiry date shown in your account" (config values: plan tokens 3 months, top-ups 12 months, server grants 90 days). State the numbers publicly?
5. **Failure charging.** Text promises only "normally not charged, fair-use limit"; settings could change the 0% partial charge. Confirm the public wording matches `cyrus_tok_failed_charge_pct=0`, `cyrus_tok_free_failures=3`.
6. **Retention periods.** Not stated by code for: server/security logs, support tickets, build records of non-erased accounts (kept indefinitely today?), project history after account closure, prompts at the AI provider. Decide and state numbers.
7. **Public mod licence.** Today: non-exclusive licence to Nxeon and users to view/download/fork/remix "as the listing allows", ownership kept. Decide whether published mods should carry a specific open licence (MIT/CC-BY/all-rights-reserved choice per mod).
8. **Community library grant.** Text says Cyrus-authored assets may be added under a CC0 dedication after filters. This is the "ToS licence grant (lawyer)" item in the tracker; confirm it is what you want, whether contributing is opt-out, and whether the flag `CYRUS_LIBRARY_CONTRIBUTE` is to be on at launch (the text is written conditional on it being on).
9. **Anthropic terms.** Confirm the AI provider's data use terms (zero data retention / no training on API data) and whether to name them as "no training" in the policy; the text only says Anthropic processes data on our behalf.
10. **Cloudflare, email provider.** Cloudflare is listed as a processor (zone cyrus.my is planned in CYRUS-MY-LAUNCH.md, not yet live); name the email provider if desired.
11. **International transfers / data location.** Primary is OVH (region?), build host node-2 is Hetzner FSN1 (Germany). Text is generic; name regions and the transfer mechanism if EU/UK users are targeted.
12. **Age.** Used 18+ from Nxeon Terms. Decide if Cyrus should allow 13-17 with a guardian (Minecraft audience is young; this is a real product risk).
13. **Account deletion deletes Google data.** Assumes deleting the Nxeon account removes `googleId`, avatar; verify in the Nxeon account-delete flow.
14. **Google OAuth verification mechanics.** The OAuth client redirect URI is on nxeon.cloud (`/api/auth/google/callback`) and Cyrus signs in through it. In the Google console the privacy policy URL must be on an authorised domain: decide between https://cyrus.my/legal/privacy (add cyrus.my as an authorised domain and verify it) or https://nxeon.cloud/legal/cyrus-privacy. Also the home page URL.
15. **Terms acceptance record** (below).

## Gaps found (not built, by design)
- **No legal acceptance versioning exists** in the platform (grep for termsVersion/acceptedTerms/LegalAcceptance found only offers/team/partner-invite acceptance, unrelated). Sign-in and sign-up only show "By continuing you agree to the Terms and the Privacy Policy" with links. I did not build a second system. To capture acceptance per version one needs a new field (for example `User.cyrusTermsVersion`/`cyrusTermsAcceptedAt`, or a row in the Cyrus consent snapshot `CyrusAttribution`), a migration (owner approval) and an interstitial at first Cyrus use. `CYRUS_LEGAL.version` is exported for that.
- **Sitemap**: no cyrus.my sitemap exists on main (the SEO agent's host-aware one is on `feature/cyrus-seo-tech`). Integration: import `CYRUS_LEGAL_SITEMAP_PATHS` and `CYRUS_LEGAL.lastmod` from `lib/legal/cyrus` and emit them with `https://cyrus.my` as the origin. The nxeon.cloud copies are deliberately NOT added to the Nxeon sitemap (canonical points to cyrus.my). The sign-in links and the landing already link /legal/terms and /legal/privacy.
- Privacy policy says "Request export"/"Request deletion" are under Account > Data: confirm the label of the page on cyrus.my once the customer app (`CYRUS_CUSTOMER_APP`) is on; deletion actually runs only when `CYRUS_GDPR_ERASE=1` and the jobs worker is on.
- Statements about "Learning data" assume `CYRUS_LEARNING` behaviours stay outcome-only.
- Withdrawing consent without a new sign-in does not stop an already queued conversion (open item R49-4/consent in docs 08); the policy says consent can be withdrawn.
- Nxeon legal nav (nxeon.cloud /legal/*) does not list the Cyrus documents (adding to the shared `LegalDoc` nav would 404 on cyrus.my frame); link from the /cyrus and /mods/cyrus marketing pages is a follow-up.
