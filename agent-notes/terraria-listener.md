## Mitigations ranked
1. Auto-restart (built, default OFF): `TERRARIA_AUTO_RESTART=1` on the worker env enables the existing sweep restart for Terraria only; cooldown `TERRARIA_AUTO_RESTART_COOLDOWN_MIN` (default 60, floor 15), time-down threshold `TERRARIA_RESTART_AFTER_MIN` (default 10, floor 4 = two sweeps). Alternative with zero code: set GameRuntime.autoRestart=true on the three rows (1h/10min). Fixes: customers locked out for hours. Does not fix: ~10-14 min outage (sweep 2 min + threshold) per death and a world restart kicks online players (note they cannot be reconnected anyway; existing sessions survive the dead listener until restart). On i2ydef the scanner will kill it again within ~4 min of every restart; with the 15 min floor that is a loop of short outages, so also do 3.
2. Dedicated IP i2ydef: block the scanner source (165.154.172.223) and anything else that hits 7777 with no player behind it, using the existing guest iptables chain (worker/firewall.ts NXEON_FW via customer firewall rules) - fixes this scanner, not the next. Only forward/allow tcp 7777 (+22) on that guest (default-drop) - cuts exposure to other ports; does not stop scanners that hit 7777.
3. Rate-limit new connections per source (hashlimit/recent) on 7777: weak. The scanner is ~6/min and the hazard is per connection, so a limit would need to be ~1/min per source to matter; real players behind carrier NAT reconnect in bursts. Not recommended except as a per-source drop-list.
4. In-guest watchdog (systemd timer: no LISTEN in /proc/net/tcp -> restart terraria.service) - 1 min recovery independent of the worker; needs a guest-template change (not built: touches live guests).
5. TShock config: nothing relevant (no connection-rate setting in config.json that I know of; not read). MaxSlots 8 keeps the blast radius small. Newer/older build: see above, no evidence.
Recommendation: ship branch, enable TERRARIA_AUTO_RESTART=1 (after merge) AND add the source block for i2ydef; consider reporting upstream (comment on tModLoader 5470 with our 1.4.5.8/TShock evidence; open a TShock issue) - owner call, I sent nothing.
