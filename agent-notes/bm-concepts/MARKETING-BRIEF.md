## Effects (owner asked for them — but read this)
The owner has rejected hero animations before as "tacky, very cheap, vibe-coded, not creative". So effects must be restrained, purposeful and premium:
- Scroll-reveal: sections and cards fade-up 12px once as they enter (IntersectionObserver, staggered 40–60ms in a grid), never re-trigger.
- The hero's three renders: a one-time staggered rise on load; on hover each lifts ~6px with its glow brightening; a very slow (≥40s) parallax/drift of the aurora plate is OK.
- Cards: hover lift 2–3px + the render scales 1.03 inside its well.
- Logo showcase: see below; a slow seamless marquee is acceptable ONLY if it stays calm — otherwise a static row of large glass tiles.
- No particles, no confetti, no blinking, no typewriter, no glitch, no neon flicker, no gradient-shifting text, no spinning.
- All motion off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. No layout shift.
