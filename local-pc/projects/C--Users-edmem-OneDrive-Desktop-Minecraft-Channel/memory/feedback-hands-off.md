When the Minecraft replay-mod setup got confusing the creator said they were "totally lost" and that the editor "needs to be full autonomous": they just play and a finished, edited video with sound effects, funny edits and B-roll appears.

**Why:** they are a creator, not a developer; every manual step (mod menus, commands, coordinates) loses them.
**How to apply:**
- Explain in plain words with short numbered steps; avoid jargon and don't ask them to learn tool internals.
- Prefer doing the work myself (read their files, check configs read-only, run tests) over asking them to click; when a step needs them, give the exact clicks and ask for a screenshot if unsure.
- Keep their asks small (close the game, say "go"). Real game launches need their explicit "go" and a PC left alone.
- Be upfront about what is proven vs untested; they appreciate honest status and correcting my own earlier mistakes (e.g. a first lag test that was wrong).
- Downloads and installs: always state file, source and size and wait for a yes (they approved CFR, Fabric API, and building the custom mod).
